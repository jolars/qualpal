# What is Qualpal?

Qualpal generates color palettes for categorical data. If you’re making a chart
with one color per group, Qualpal chooses colors that are easy to tell apart.
It measures how different colors look and tries to make even the closest pair
in the palette as distinct as possible.

You choose how many colors you need and where Qualpal can look for them. Give
it a range of hues, saturations, and lightnesses, supply your own colors, or
start from a built-in palette. You can keep existing colors when adding groups,
account for color vision deficiencies, and choose colors that stand out against
your chart’s background.

Try the [browser app](app/index.md), or follow [Getting
started](../docs/getting_started.md) to generate a palette with the C++ library.
You can also use the [command-line tool](command-line.md) or the R and Python
packages listed in the API menu.
